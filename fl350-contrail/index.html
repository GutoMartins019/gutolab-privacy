English

Effective date: 2026-10-29

This policy covers the FL350 Contrail watch face for Wear OS, published by Guto Lab.

Data we collect

None. FL350 Contrail does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data shown on the watch face

The watch face can display the time, your heart rate, your step count and the battery level. Wear OS reads these values on your watch and passes them to the watch face for display. They never leave the watch, and Guto Lab never receives them.

Network access

The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It has no accounts or sign-in.

Children

The app is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
